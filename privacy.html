Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 19, 2026

This website, ubersoft.pro (the "site"), is published by Ubersoft LLC ("we," "us," "our"). The short version: this is a static informational site — it sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and does not track you.

01 — Information we collect

None through the site itself. There are no accounts, no forms, no comment sections, and no advertising. We do not collect, store, or sell personal information from your visit.

02 — Hosting

The site is served by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). Like any web host, GitHub may receive standard technical data — such as your IP address in server logs — in order to deliver and secure the service. That data is handled under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

03 — Fonts

The site loads the Chakra Petch typeface from Google Fonts. Your browser requests those font files directly from Google, which may log the request (including your IP address) under Google's privacy policy. No other third-party resources are loaded.

Apps we publish have their own privacy policies — in every app below, your data stays entirely on your device:

05 — Changes

If we ever change how the site handles information, we will update this page and the effective date above.

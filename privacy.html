Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 1, 2026

This website, ubersoft.pro (the "site"), is published by Ubersoft LLC ("we," "us," "our"). The short version: this is a static informational site — it sets no cookies, has no accounts and no advertising, and counts visits with a privacy-focused analytics service that cannot identify you.

01 — Information we collect

Nothing that identifies you. There are no accounts, no forms, no comment sections, and no advertising. We do not collect, store, or sell personal information from your visit. The site does count page views, in the anonymous form described in section 03.

02 — Hosting

The site is served by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). Like any web host, GitHub may receive standard technical data — such as your IP address in server logs — in order to deliver and secure the service. That data is handled under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

03 — Analytics

This site does count visits — including this page. It uses GoatCounter, a privacy-focused analytics service, to record which pages get read and roughly which country readers arrive from. It sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser. It does not keep your IP address, does not follow you across other sites, and never builds a profile of you. Your browser contacts GoatCounter directly, so it can see your IP address at the moment of the request.

04 — What else the site loads

Nothing else. Typefaces, images and scripts are served from ubersoft.pro itself — no font host, no CDN, no advertising or social network is contacted. Apart from GoatCounter above and the host below, your browser makes no third-party requests on this site.

05 — Our products

Apps we publish have their own privacy policies — in every app below, your data stays entirely on your device:

  • Zombie Bill Tracker — zbilltracker.com/privacy
  • Invasion Of Zombies — invasionofzombies.com/privacy
  • The App Lockr — theapplockr.com/privacy
  • Wave: Check-In — wavetoday.app/privacy

06 — Changes

If we ever change how the site handles information, we will update this page and the effective date above.

07 — Contact

Questions? Email info@ubersoft.pro. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you send — we use it only to respond to you, and we never share it, sell it, or add you to a mailing list.
